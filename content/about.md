### Co-founder & CEO of OpsCompanion

I am the co-founder and CEO of [OpsCompanion](https://opscompanion.ai/), an AI security company. We build Standpipe, which runs on the device, shows a company every AI tool operating across its machines, and governs what each one is allowed to see, send, and do.

Sovereign AI means you own and you know the data going into a model and the data coming out of it, no matter the model type. That is the whole definition. The useful way to hold it is as a supply chain problem, the same way you would think about anything else where integrity matters, because if you cannot account for every step then you cannot promise that no one else has been in the chain. We spent about twenty years pretending this was fine for software and got a world built out of libraries maintained by one exhausted person on a weekend. AI multiplies that, and then adds a context window that can be steered by a single changed word.

The part I care about most is what this does to ordinary people. A capable model attached to your actual life, your money and your health and your family, is worth an enormous amount, and getting there means handing it your whole context. Nobody should do that when the box belongs to someone else. Security is the precondition for AI reaching regular people at all, and right now it is the least glamorous and least funded corner of the field.

I wrote the argument up in [Go West, Nerd.](/blog/go-west-nerd/) on June 2nd, 2026. Google published [Beyond Zero](https://doi.org/10.1145/3819083) in ACM Queue two weeks later, making the case that zero trust falls apart under AI agents and authorization has to shrink down to the individual action. Cloudflare followed in August with [the Agent Access Model](https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-agent-access-model/), making the case that enforcement has to live in the harness and the network instead of the prompt. Both are right and both are worth your time. Where I go further is on ownership, because a rented enforcement layer is still rented, and that is why Standpipe enforces on hardware you already own.
